How games are judged

Succeeded: Halloween: at least 3 guests voted and at least half of the invited guests voted. Matched: the host ran the reveal to the final scores and at least 2 couples answered in full.

Played: at least one guest took part, but it didn't meet the bar above. Not played: the game was set up but nobody took part.

Took part counts guests who submitted; invited counts names the host added.
